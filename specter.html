    Stowed, the 20 tiles are the hexagonal faces of a truncated icosahedron; the 12 pentagonal gaps stay open as listening ports. Same tiles, same edge length, laid flat they make the 5 m aperture from the hull derivation. In hinged mode the same tiles unfold as a net: hexagons only share alternate edges, so the net is a three-lobed honeycomb with tile-sized holes. Wider than the filled array, so a narrower main beam, but thinned, so stronger sidelobes. Three horns look out through the holes. Dots on the tiles stand in for the real element lattice, which is far denser.

    Stowed, the 20 tiles are the hexagonal faces of a truncated icosahedron; the 12 pentagonal gaps stay open as listening ports. Same tiles, same edge length, laid flat they make the 5 m aperture from the hull derivation. In hinged mode all 20 tiles unfold as a net: hexagons only share alternate edges, so the net is a three-armed pinwheel with open notches between the arms, and the aft tile rides out on the end of one arm. About the same radiating area as the filled array, so about the same on-axis intensity; the main lobe is roughly 30% narrower but holds about half the power instead of 83%, with the rest in sidelobes. The horns rise on posts until their mouths are level with the array face. Dots on the tiles stand in for the real element lattice, which is far denser.

    Stowed, the 20 tiles are the hexagonal faces of a truncated icosahedron; the 12 pentagonal gaps stay open as listening ports. Same tiles, same edge length, laid flat they make the 5 m aperture from the hull derivation. Dots on the tiles stand in for the real element lattice, which is far denser.
